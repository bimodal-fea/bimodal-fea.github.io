### Background
I hold an M.Sc. (2012) and B.Sc. (2010) in Aerospace Engineering from Purdue University. For over 12 years, I have worked in the aerospace industry as a structural analyst, specializing in Finite Element Analysis (FEA) of complex designs. My career has included roles at Northrop Grumman, Lockheed Martin, and L3Harris, where I have worked on a wide range of hardware, including gas turbine engines, aircraft, missiles, and satellite systems. My analysis experience spans loads, stress, dynamics, optimization, and fatigue.

FEA works by discretizing 3D geometry into elements so that designs can be evaluated numerically for stress, modal response, dynamics, thermal behavior, and more. Depending on model size, this can generate enormous volumes of data. To manage it, I rely on MATLAB, Python, and Excel to wrangle, organize, and visualize structural analysis results.

Working in aerospace and defense means tackling some of the most demanding engineering problems, but it also means nearly all of my work stays behind secure walls. After more than a decade in the field, I wanted something tangible to demonstrate my expertise. This technical portfolio is the result: a showcase of my skills and my understanding of structural analysis.
